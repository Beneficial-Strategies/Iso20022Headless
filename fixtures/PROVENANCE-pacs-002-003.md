# Provenance of the pacs.002.001.16 and pacs.003.001.12 fixtures

Captured 2026-10-06 from the **staging** ISO 20022 MCP, with the method of `PROVENANCE-pain-007-to-018.md`
(`tools/spec-extract` from `get_spec_snapshot`, plus targeted lookups).

## Which messages
Asked for "pacs.001 and pacs.002". **There is no pacs.001 in the repository.** Reading the identifier of every pacs family's
latest version gives: 002 (FIToFIPaymentStatusReport V16), 003 (FIToFICustomerDirectDebit V12), 004 (PaymentReturn V15),
006 (superseded, see below), 007 (FIToFIPaymentReversal V14), 008 (FIToFICustomerCreditTransfer V14), 009
(FinancialInstitutionCreditTransfer V13), 010 (FinancialInstitutionDirectDebit V06), 028 (FIToFIPaymentStatusRequest V07),
029 (MultilateralSettlementRequest V02). The request was put to the user, who chose **pacs.003** to go with pacs.002.
`PaymentStatusReportV02` in the same area is pacs.002.001.02, an early version of the pacs.002 family.

## What was new
Almost everything is shared with the pain messages: pacs.002 adds 4 components and 1 simple type, pacs.003 adds 5 components and
2 code sets (214 types are now shared by two or more messages).

## New rule features these messages needed
- `EqualToNode` / `DifferentFromNode`: compare one field with another (the currency of an amount with the currency of another).
- The path `/A/@Currency` (the currency attribute of an amount).
- A literal `sum of /A/B`: the group total must equal the exact sum of all occurrences (decimal arithmetic, so 0.1 + 0.2 = 0.3).
Without these, three pacs.003 rules and the sum rule would have been reported as unsupported.
Behavior tests: `packages/validate/test/rules-pacs.test.ts` and the node/sum cases in `rules.test.ts`.
