## New rule features these messages needed
- `number of occurrences of X` / `Number Occurrences X`: the number of transactions must match the transactions given.
- `substring(/A/B,start,length)`: the first characters of a text (positions count from 1), used to forbid cover settlement for returns of direct debits.
- `sum of A/B` written without a leading slash.
- Value kind `time` (`ISOTime`, `hh:mm:ss`, optional fraction and zone).
- A safety guard: a rule that names a field the message does not have is reported **unsupported** (`rule_path_unknown`), never guessed.

## Known limits
- **Two spec typos.** `PaymentReturnV15.TotalReturnedInterbankSettlementAmountAndSumRule` and
  `FIToFIPaymentReversalV14.TotalReversedInterbankSettlementAmountAndSumRule` spell the field `...InterbankSttlementAmount` in their
  expressions. They are reported unsupported, with the reason; they are listed in `KNOWN_SPEC_TYPOS` (`rule-paths.test.ts`).
- `PaymentInstruction50/51.ChargesAccountAgentRule` compare with a pseudo-literal (unsupported, as in pain.013).
- Seven rules exist only as prose in the spec (see `rulecoverage.test.ts`).
- 320 rules in all: 232 machine-checked (each shown able to pass and to fail), 4 unsupported, the rest prose-only.
- Spanish text for the new units is machine-drafted, marked "machine translated".
